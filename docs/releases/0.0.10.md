# Specification 0.0.10: release notes

Specification [0.0.10](../../spec/0.0.10/archive-spec.md) installs the
contracts of ADRs 0079 to 0089 beside every 0.0.9 bundle with its exact
bytes: harmony as claims, keys and tuning, scoped judgments and span
annotations, form, lead sheets, documents and timed lyrics, sensitivity and
rights, notes and melody, tracklists and catalogue correspondence, the
circle's work origin and sharing consent, and exports. Section D-18 states
their rules. The layout is `archive/2` and the core context is 0.0.9's, so a
0.0.9 archive adopts it by the specification upgrade.

## Amended before release (2026-10-19)

The operator accepted three amendments before 0.0.10 was cut. Because the
release is not yet out, the bundles were changed in place and their earlier
draft digests were never released.

- **Articulation, written rhythm, and the bass line**
  ([ADR 0086](../decisions/0086-notes-and-melody.md)): notes and Work-level
  melody pitches carry optional `articulation`; a notes claim may say `bass`;
  written rhythm and the bass line are World views.
- **Syllables and the notes that carry them**
  ([ADR 0085](../decisions/0085-documents-lyrics-rights.md)): a timed word
  may hold syllables by character offsets, each optionally carried by notes
  of a pinned notes claim; machine timing is forced alignment.
- **The check beyond the circle**
  ([ADR 0085](../decisions/0085-documents-lyrics-rights.md)): publication
  approval version 2 states its class, and Door refuses one beyond the
  circle that reach or the sharing gate forbids; World's `may_publish` says
  whether an approval still permits publication. A version 1 approval
  reaches no further than the circle. No publisher ships.

Every 0.0.9 bundle keeps its bytes. The rule forms the amendments add are
`x-archive-sequential-spans` and `x-archive-contained-spans` as lists with
nested paths, and `x-archive-beyond-circle` (D-18).

Also confirmed by the operator on 2026-10-19
([ADR 0088](../decisions/0088-bandmates.md)): a member keeps seeing how many
things a view withheld from them (INV-24), and the rule that a supersession
keeps its agent applies only to records under `archive/0.0.10` or later.

## Decided while implementing

The operator accepted these choices, made while building 0.0.10, as built
(2026-10-19):

- **Relay codes.** `render_failed` (502, practice renders),
  `export_failed` (502, `POST /exports`), and `export_input_unknown` (404,
  `POST /exports` and `GET /exports/{key}`).
- **Operator-only views.** `readable_set` and `reach` answer the operator
  only.
- **Shared content.** Content a member reads needs every Artifact that
  carries it to be readable.
- **What an exporter reads.** An exporter is given the readable closure of
  what it renders.
- **Staging.** The relay stages uploaded documents in a git-annex
  repository under `staging/`.
- **World's views.** Eight views beyond the records' own: `standing`,
  `reach`, `readable_set`, `lead_sheet_changes`, `played_sections`,
  `played_bars`, `tune`, and `track_credits`; and key-aware spelling, a key
  parameter of `chord_names` and `chords_current`.
- **Door.** A chord sequence version 2 is related to what it was made from
  by `derived_from`.
- **Proposers.** The bass proposer's thresholds are pitched at least 0.5
  and lead at least 0.2; tuning drift is a change of more than 10
  cents, with overlapping stretches merged; weights are written with four
  decimals; `propose --all` answers `{status, artifacts}`.

Made while implementing the amendments, for the operator to confirm:

- **Bleed and the bass line in enrich.** The vocal line's bleed filter is
  the `notes` proposer's, not the `melody` extractor's: a note more than 7
  semitones under the duration-weighted 10th percentile of the run's
  pitches, or of the bass line's class and within an octave of a bass note
  (12 semitones) for half its length while the vocal stem is more than
  20 dB under its loud level; the run's settings count what each rule
  dropped. The `bass-line` proposer keeps notes by the bass proposer's
  thresholds. The `timed-lyrics` proposer refuses with code `not_yet` until
  an aligner is installed.
- **Notes from a pitch track.** The `notes` proposer (version 3) builds the
  vocal line from `f0-pesto` of the vocal stem, and `bass-line` (version 2)
  the bass line from `f0-pesto` of the bass stem, with one note builder
  (`enrich/note_builder.py`): one note per held pitch, slides and vibrato
  inside the note, a gap under 100 ms on one pitch bridged for a voice and
  never for a bass, a 3 dB level dip splitting a bass note, notes from
  60 ms (voice) or 30 ms (bass), pitch against the recording's tuning,
  onsets unshifted (on real separated single lines they sat 7 to 14 ms
  early; a synthetic 55 ms bias did not hold). Without `f0-pesto` the
  vocal line is `melody/notes` and the bass line Basic Pitch's notes, as
  before. `f0-pesto` version 2 adds
  `level_db` and `loud_db` for the builder.
- **Written rhythm, version 1.** Four grid points per beat; a note is
  `staccato` when it sounds less than 0.6 of the interval to the next
  onset; a rest needs a silence of at least one beat at a `line_end` or a
  `breath`, and where no timed lyrics line covers the silence it counts as
  a breath.
- **`may_publish` is operator-only** in the relay, like `reach`.
- **Projection.** Projection 6 moves to revision 7 for the amendments'
  terms. A withdrawal's `reason` projects as `arch:withdrawal_reason`,
  because `arch:reason` already belongs to judgments.

Approved by the operator on 2026-10-19, after an album trial of keys:

- **No machine tonic.** `tonal` 6 names no tonic: `tonic_named` is false
  everywhere, and every `keys` and `key_regions` span is labelled with its
  note collection and valued by the collection's margin. Version 5's
  confidence did not rank (Brier 0.316 against a constant's 0.247) and 7 of
  its 12 named tonics disagreed with the published keys. The `key` proposer
  (version 2) therefore proposes collections only; readers use `collection`
  and `scale_notes`. The tonic is to be revisited only with references
  that settle relative major and minor.

Fixed after the real-data rehearsals (2026-10-19), for the operator to
confirm:

- **Refusals say why.** A schema refusal names where the instance fails
  and the rule it breaks (`contract instance does not satisfy its schema:
  /payload/kind breaks enum of the contract schema at payload.kind`), through
  the relay too. Door's command line follows its `door: <message>` stderr
  line with the service's JSON reply ([ADR 0058](../decisions/0058-door-refusal-codes.md),
  amended); a refusal still writes no stdout.
- **Door's day.** World's views and the relay read rights and reach on the
  UTC date, as Door's check beyond the circle does, never the host's local
  date.
- **A lead sheet's recording.** `lead_sheet_current` with a `recording`
  reads a lead sheet that pins it as `recording` or, pinning none, as its
  `source` ([ADR 0082](../decisions/0082-views-and-current.md), amended).
- **The backfill.** `enrich propose --all` lists under `unprobed` the audio
  or video Artifacts with no audio stream claim and says to run `enrich
  probe` first; it does not probe for you. `extract.py` reads the media
  type of suffix-less archive content from its first bytes.

Not built in 0.0.10, though the amendments describe them:

- **Exporters.** No exporter in this release writes syllables under their
  notes, articulation, or the bass line's own staff; the exporters are
  installed programs, and the amended ADRs 0085, 0086, and 0089 say what one
  must write.
- **Forced alignment.** No aligner is installed, so no machine timed lyrics
  claim has syllables yet.
- **A publisher.** None ships; one must ask `may_publish` before it sends.
